# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.
Be constructive, assume good intent, respect different levels of experience,
and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted
sexual attention, publishing another person's private information, or sustained
disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when behavior violates this standard. Report conduct concerns privately to the
repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed
confidentially and handled in a way appropriate to the impact and context.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in
public community spaces.
